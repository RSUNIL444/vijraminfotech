VIJRAM Jobs Lite App FAQs

Find quick answers about registration, app usage, job matching, and support.

General App Usage

What is VIJRAM Jobs Lite?

VIJRAM Jobs Lite is a mobile job portal designed for unskilled and semi-skilled workers seeking nearby opportunities. With location-based matching, candidates can quickly apply for roles such as factory helper, drivers, accountants, female helpers, housekeeping staff, janitors, warehouse workers, farmworkers, food service workers, retail sales associates, dishwashers, groundskeepers, cleaners, delivery drivers, cashiers, construction laborers, material handlers, landscaping laborers, machine operators, assembly line workers, production workers, laundry workers, stock clerks, and parking lot attendants. The app simplifies job hunting with one-tap applications and instant profile setup.

How do I register on the app?

Registration is quick and simple. Enter your mobile number, verify with OTP, and complete your profile with basic details like name, age, and gender. Sharing your location helps us match you with jobs near your area.

Is the app free to use?

Yes, VIJRAM Jobs Lite is completely free. There are no charges for registration, applying to jobs, or using any of the app’s features.

Can I use the app without sharing my location?

No. Location sharing is essential for matching candidates with nearby job openings. It ensures you only receive relevant opportunities close to your work or residence.

How do I download the app?

You can download VIJRAM Jobs Lite directly from the Google Play Store. Search for “VIJRAM Jobs Lite” and tap Install. Once installed, open the app, register with your mobile number, and start applying for jobs instantly.

Profile & Account

How do I complete my profile?

Complete your profile by verifying your mobile number with OTP, then adding details such as name, age, gender, and preferred job type. Your GPS location helps us connect you with suitable openings nearby.

Can I edit my details later?

Currently, profile details cannot be edited after submission. You may reapply after 60 days to refresh your information and move your application to the top of our database.

Do I need to upload a resume?

No resume is required for most unskilled and semi-skilled roles. Basic profile information is sufficient. If needed, our team may request a resume later for specific job categories.

How do I reset my password or change mobile number?

The app does not use passwords. You can reapply using your mobile number and OTP verification. Reapplying after 60 days refreshes your profile and improves visibility in our system.

Job Search & Applications

How does location-based job matching work?

Our team matches candidates with jobs based on GPS location. When employers share openings, our system identifies nearby candidates and connects them directly, ensuring faster and more relevant placements.

What types of jobs are available on the app?

VIJRAM Jobs Lite does not display job listings directly. Instead, candidates looking for unskilled and semi-skilled jobs can register by selecting their preferred job type. Our system then matches profiles with openings shared by employers near their location. Job categories include factory helpers, drivers, accountants, female helpers, housekeeping staff, janitors, warehouse workers, farmworkers, food service workers, retail sales associates, dishwashers, groundskeepers, cleaners, delivery drivers, cashiers, construction laborers, material handlers, landscaping laborers, machine operators, assembly line workers, production workers, laundry workers, stock clerks, and parking lot attendants.

How do I apply for a job with one tap?

Once your profile is complete, you can apply instantly with a single tap. Your application is saved in our database and matched with nearby openings by our back-office team.

Support & Troubleshooting

Why am I not receiving the OTP?

Ensure you have stable network coverage and restart your phone if needed. If the issue persists, try again after a few minutes or contact support.

What should I do if the app crashes?

If the app crashes, note the steps that caused the issue and report them to us at vijram.infotech@gmail.com so our team can assist you.

How do I contact support for help?

You can reach our support team by emailing vijram.infotech@gmail.com. We’ll respond promptly to resolve your queries.

Is my personal data secure on the app?

Yes. Your personal data is stored securely and used only for job matching purposes. We follow strict privacy practices to protect candidate information.
