Job Seeker & Employer FAQs

Find answers to common questions about finding jobs, applying for vacancies, managing your profile, and staying safe during your job search on Vijram Jobs.

Job Seeker FAQs

What is Vijram Jobs?

Vijram Jobs is an online job portal that helps job seekers discover employment opportunities across different industries, job roles, and locations. Candidates can explore job listings, review job requirements, and apply for suitable opportunities through the portal.

Is Vijram Jobs free for job seekers?

Yes, job seekers can browse job listings and use the available job-seeking features without paying a fee, unless a specific service is expressly identified as paid. Candidates should never pay an individual claiming that payment is required to guarantee a job through Vijram Jobs.

Do I need to register to search for jobs?

You can browse available job listings on Vijram Jobs without necessarily creating an account. However, registration or login may be required to access certain features, such as applying for jobs or managing your profile.

How do I create an account on Vijram Jobs?

Visit the Vijram Jobs website and navigate to the registration or sign-up section. Enter the requested information and complete any verification steps required to activate your account.

Always provide accurate and up-to-date contact information so that employers can reach you regarding suitable opportunities.

How can I search for jobs on Vijram Jobs?

You can explore job listings based on available search options, such as job title, skills, company, industry, or location. Review the job description, qualifications, experience requirements, salary information, and work location before applying.

How do I apply for a job?

Open the job listing that interests you and review its requirements. If you meet the eligibility criteria, click the available application button and follow the instructions.

Depending on the vacancy, you may need to log in, complete your profile, upload your resume, or provide additional information requested by the employer.

Can I apply for multiple jobs?

Yes. You can apply for multiple vacancies that match your qualifications, skills, and career interests, subject to the application requirements specified for each listing.

We recommend tailoring your resume and application details to the requirements of each position.

Do I need a resume to apply for jobs?

A resume is recommended because it helps employers understand your qualifications, skills, experience, and career objectives. Some employers may require a resume as part of the application process.

Keep your resume updated and ensure that the information provided is accurate.

Can freshers apply for jobs on Vijram Jobs?

Yes. Freshers can explore suitable entry-level positions, trainee roles, internships, apprenticeships, and other opportunities where available.

Check the experience requirements in each job listing. Look for positions that accept freshers or candidates with no prior work experience.

Can I find jobs based on my location?

Yes. You can explore job opportunities based on the locations specified in job listings. Depending on the available vacancies, you may find opportunities in your city, nearby industrial areas, or other locations.

Always verify the job location and work arrangement before applying.

Will Vijram Jobs guarantee me a job?

No. Vijram Jobs does not guarantee employment, interviews, or selection. Hiring decisions are made by employers based on their recruitment requirements and the suitability of candidates.

We provide a platform to help candidates discover employment opportunities and connect with prospective employers.

Does Vijram Jobs charge candidates for interviews or job offers?

No candidate should pay money to anyone claiming that payment is required to guarantee a job, interview, selection, or offer through Vijram Jobs.

Be cautious of individuals who demand registration fees, interview fees, security deposits, processing charges, or other payments in exchange for employment.

Do not transfer money merely because someone claims to represent Vijram Jobs or an employer. Verify suspicious requests through independently obtained official contact details before taking any action.

How can I identify a fake job or recruitment scam?

Watch out for warning signs such as:

  • Requests for money in exchange for a guaranteed job or interview.
  • Offers made without a reasonable recruitment or selection process.
  • Unusually high salaries for minimal qualifications or experience.
  • Requests for OTPs, passwords, banking credentials, or unnecessary sensitive information.
  • Communication from suspicious email addresses or unofficial phone numbers.
  • Pressure to make immediate payments or share confidential documents.

Always verify the employer, job details, and communication before proceeding. A listing on a job portal should not be treated as an absolute guarantee that an employer or vacancy is genuine.

What should I do if someone asks me for money for a job?

Do not make the payment. Preserve relevant evidence, such as messages, phone numbers, emails, payment requests, and transaction details.

Report the suspicious job listing or communication to Vijram Jobs through the available contact or reporting channel. If you have already lost money to suspected recruitment fraud, contact your bank or payment provider promptly and report the incident to the appropriate authorities.

How can I report a suspicious job listing?

If you identify a job listing that appears misleading, fraudulent, or suspicious, contact Vijram Jobs through the official contact details provided on the website. Include the job title, listing link, employer details, and a description of your concern.

We may review reported listings and take appropriate action in accordance with our policies. Reporting a listing does not guarantee a particular outcome.

Is my personal information safe on Vijram Jobs?

Vijram Jobs handles personal information in accordance with its Privacy Policy and applicable laws. Information you provide may be processed to operate the platform, facilitate recruitment-related services, and support interactions with employers.

Review your profile and application details before submitting them. Avoid sharing passwords, OTPs, banking credentials, or other confidential information with recruiters or unknown individuals.

For more information, please refer to the Privacy Policy available on our website.

Can I update my profile after registration?

Yes, where profile management is available, you can log in to your account and update your personal details, qualifications, skills, experience, and other relevant information.

Keeping your profile accurate and up to date can help employers better assess your suitability for available positions.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the password recovery or forgot-password option on the login page, if available. Follow the instructions to verify your identity and reset your password.

Never share your password or OTP with anyone claiming to provide recruitment assistance.

How can I contact Vijram Jobs for support?

If you have questions about your account, job applications, suspicious listings, or the use of the portal, contact Vijram Jobs through the official contact details or support channels provided on the website.

Employer / Recruiter FAQs

How do I register as an employer?

Click on Employer Registration, fill in your company and contact details, verify your mobile number/email using OTP, and create your account.

Is employer registration free?

Yes. Employer registration is completely free.

How many jobs can I post for free?

Employers can post up to 2 job vacancies per month free of charge. Additional posting plans may be introduced in the future.

What information is required to post a job?

Typically, you need to provide:

  • Job Title
  • Job Description
  • Required Skills
  • Experience Level
  • Salary Range (Optional)
  • Job Location
How long does it take for my job to become visible?

Jobs are published immediately after submission.

Can I edit a job after posting?

Yes. Employers can edit job details, requirements, salary information, and application deadlines at any time before the job is closed.

Can I close a job posting before its expiry date?

Yes. You can close or deactivate a job posting whenever the recruitment requirement is fulfilled.

How can I view applicants for my job?

Log in to your employer account, navigate to Manage Jobs, and select applications for the relevant job posting to view all applications received.

Can I view the applicant profile?

Yes. You can view an applicant's profile. Click the applicant's Profile to view their details.

Can I shortlist candidates?

Yes. You can shortlist relevant candidates and manage applications efficiently through the portal.

How do candidates apply for jobs?

Job seekers can apply directly through the website by submitting their profile and resume.

Can I post jobs for multiple locations?

Yes. Employers can create separate postings for different branches, offices, or locations.

What types of jobs can be posted?

You can post:

  • Full-Time Jobs
  • Part-Time Jobs
  • Contract Jobs
  • Internship Opportunities
  • Temporary Positions
  • Remote Jobs (if applicable)
Is there a limit on the number of applicants?

No. There is no restriction on the number of applications you can receive for a posted job.

How can I update my company details?

Go to Company Profile in your employer dashboard and update your company information, contact details, and website.

What should I do if I forget my password?

Use the Forgot Password option on the login page and follow the OTP/email verification process to reset your password.

Can recruitment agencies register as employers?

Yes. Recruitment agencies can register and post job opportunities on behalf of their clients, subject to platform policies.

What types of jobs are not allowed?

The following are prohibited:

  • Fake or misleading jobs
  • Jobs involving illegal activities
  • Discriminatory job postings
  • Fraudulent work-from-home schemes
  • Any opportunity violating applicable laws
