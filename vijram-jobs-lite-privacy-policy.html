VIJRAM Jobs Lite Android Application

Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

Vijram Infotech ("we," "us," or "our") operates the VIJRAM Jobs Lite mobile application (the "App"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect information when you use the App to register for employment opportunities.

1. Information We Collect

We collect only the information needed to register you, understand your job preferences, and connect you with suitable opportunities. This may include:

  • Mobile number: Used to create your account and verify your identity with a one-time password (OTP).
  • Profile information: Details such as your name, age, gender, preferred job category, and other information you provide during registration.
  • Location information: Your current location, when you grant permission, to match you with jobs in or near your area.
  • Device and technical information: Basic information needed to operate, secure, and troubleshoot the App, such as device type, operating system, and application diagnostics.
  • Support information: Information you choose to provide when contacting us about support or an application.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Register and verify your account.
  • Match your profile with relevant employment opportunities.
  • Contact you about suitable jobs and assist with the recruitment process.
  • Respond to support requests and communicate important service information.
  • Maintain, secure, improve, and troubleshoot the App.
  • Prevent misuse, fraud, or activity that could harm users or the service.
  • Meet legal and regulatory requirements.

3. Location Information

Location access is used to provide location-based job matching. The App requests location permission for this purpose. You may manage location permission through your Android device settings; however, disabling it may prevent us from providing nearby job recommendations.

4. Sharing of Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information only when necessary to provide the service, including:

  • With employers or recruitment partners when needed to evaluate and progress a suitable job opportunity.
  • With service providers who help us operate the App, verify OTPs, store information, or provide technical support. They may use information only for the services they provide to us.
  • With authorities or other parties when required by law, legal process, or to protect the rights, safety, and security of users, our company, or the public.

5. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide recruitment services, support your account, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. When information is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymize it where reasonably possible.

6. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards to protect your information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, alteration, or disclosure. No electronic transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure, so we cannot promise absolute security.

7. Your Choices and Rights

You may choose not to provide optional information or withdraw device permissions through Android settings. You may also contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and our legitimate need to maintain records.

8. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for people who are legally able to seek employment. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided information to us, please contact us so we can review and delete it where required.

9. Third-Party Services and Links

The App may use third-party services, such as OTP delivery, hosting, analytics, or the Google Play Store. Those services may process information under their own privacy policies. We encourage you to review the policies of any third-party service you use.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our services or legal requirements change. We will post the updated policy on this page and revise the date above. Your continued use of the App after an update means that you acknowledge the revised policy.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions, requests, or concerns about this Privacy Policy or your personal information, contact Vijram Infotech at vijram.infotech@gmail.com.
