Job Seeker & Employer FAQs

Find quick answers about applying for jobs, posting openings, location filters, and support.

Job Seeker FAQs

How do I apply for jobs on this portal?

To apply, register with your email ID and password, then verify your mobile number via OTP. Once your profile is complete with details such as education, skills, and experience, you can apply directly to jobs posted on vijramjobs.in. A complete profile increases your chances of being shortlisted.

Is creating an account free?

Yes. Creating an account on vijramjobs.in is completely free for all job seekers.

What types of jobs are listed here?

The portal features openings across industries including manufacturing, IT, education, administration, sales, and service sectors. You can find jobs ranging from entry-level positions to specialized roles in multiple departments.

Employer / Recruiter FAQs

How can I post a job opening?

Our employer portal is under development. For now, you can share your job postings with our team, and we will publish them on vijramjobs.in on your behalf.

What are the charges for posting jobs?

Currently, posting jobs on vijramjobs.in is free. Employers can list openings without any charges.

How do I search for candidates by skills or location?

At present, employers can only view candidates who have applied for their posted jobs.

Location & Education FAQs

Can I search jobs by location?

Yes. You can search jobs by title, education, and location to find openings that match your preferences.

Do you list jobs by education qualification?

Yes. Each job posting includes details such as required education, qualifications, experience, skills, compensation, and work location.

Can I filter jobs by degree or specialization?

Yes. You can filter jobs by degree, specialization, and location to quickly find relevant opportunities.

Technical & Support FAQs

How do I reset my password?

You can reset your password by selecting the Reset Password option on the login page and following the instructions.

How do I contact support for issues?

For assistance, email us at vijram.infotech@gmail.com. Please include the issue type in the subject line for faster resolution.

Is there a mobile app for this portal?

No. Currently, vijramjobs.in is available only as a web portal. However, the site is fully responsive and works seamlessly on mobile browsers.
